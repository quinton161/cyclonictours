Privacy

This website does not require an account and does not collect payments. Your enquiry details are used to prepare a WhatsApp message in your browser. Nothing is sent to Cyclonic Tours until you choose to send the message in WhatsApp.

External services

WhatsApp processes messages under its own privacy terms. Fonts, photographs and interaction scripts are served by this website’s hosting provider. Some retained animation assets may load from the template’s asset provider.

Your information

Do not include passport, payment or other sensitive information in the enquiry form. Confirm how your booking information will be handled directly with Cyclonic Tours before providing it.

For questions about your enquiry, contact Cyclonic Tours on +263 77 261 9171.
