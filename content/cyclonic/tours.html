FAQ

A little clarity before the adventure.

How do I enquire about a tour?

Choose an experience, enter your preferred date, traveller count and name, then continue to WhatsApp. Send the prepared message to request availability and a quotation.

Can I request a customised tour?

Include your interests, travel dates and any special requirements in your enquiry. Ask Cyclonic Tours which arrangements are available for your visit.

How do I confirm availability?

Availability is confirmed directly by Cyclonic Tours. Sending an enquiry does not reserve a place or confirm a booking.

What should I bring?

Ask for the packing list for your chosen activity before travelling. Requirements depend on the experience, weather and itinerary.

Where is Cyclonic Tours located?

Find us at Stand 6578, Mkhosana Township, Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe. Arrange your visit or pickup before travelling.

How are payments handled?

Confirm the price, payment method, cancellation terms and booking conditions directly with Cyclonic Tours. This website does not collect payments.
